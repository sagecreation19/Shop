About Abhijatya

Our story of tradition, craftsmanship and timeless beauty

Our Story

Abhijatya was born from a deep love for India's artistic heritage. We believe that jewellery and sarees are more than accessories — they are expressions of identity, celebrations of craft, and bridges between generations.

Every piece in our collection is carefully sourced from skilled artisans across India, from the silk weavers of Kanchipuram to the kundan craftsmen of Rajasthan. We work directly with these talented makers, ensuring fair practices and preserving the traditions that make each creation unique.

Our Craft

From hand-embroidered zardozi work to intricate temple jewellery designs, every piece reflects hours of meticulous craftsmanship. Our artisans use techniques passed down through generations, blending them with contemporary aesthetics.

We take pride in quality — each item is individually inspected before it reaches you. Our sarees are woven with the finest yarns, and our jewellery is crafted using premium materials that stand the test of time.

Return & Exchange Policy

At Abhijatya, customer satisfaction is important to us. We want every purchase to bring you joy and confidence. Please read our return and exchange guidelines carefully before placing your order.

Return Eligibility

Returns may be accepted under the following circumstances:

  • You receive a damaged or defective product.
  • You receive an incorrect item.
  • The product has a verified quality issue.

All such issues must be reported within 48 hours of receiving your order.

Mandatory Unboxing Video Requirement

Recording a continuous, clear unboxing video of your order is mandatory for any return or exchange request.

  • Customers must record a video while opening their package, starting from the sealed parcel.
  • The video must clearly show the package condition, opening process, and the received product.
  • The product and any damage, defect, or incorrect item must be clearly visible.
  • The video must be unedited and uninterrupted to be considered valid.
  • The unboxing video must be submitted along with the return or exchange request.

Important: Return or exchange requests without a valid unboxing video may not be accepted. Customers are strongly advised to record their unboxing video before opening or using the product.

Exchange Policy

Exchange requests may be considered for eligible products, subject to product availability and inspection.

A valid unboxing video is compulsory for all exchange requests.

Non-Returnable Items

For hygiene and product-quality reasons, certain items may not be eligible for return or exchange, including:

  • Earrings and other eligible intimate-contact jewellery.
  • Products marked as Final Sale.
  • Customised or personalised products.
  • Products showing signs of use, damage, or alteration caused after delivery.

Sarees and other eligible products will be assessed according to their condition and the applicable return terms.

Conditions for Returns

  • Products must be unused and in their original condition.
  • Original packaging, tags, and accessories must be retained.
  • Proof of purchase or order details must be provided.
  • A clear, continuous, and unedited unboxing video is mandatory.
  • All return or exchange claims must be submitted within 48 hours of delivery.

Replacement Policy (No Cash Refunds)

Abhijatya does not offer cash refunds or monetary reimbursements for returned products.

  • Once a return request is approved after product inspection and verification of the submitted evidence, the customer will receive a replacement of the same product.
  • Replacement is subject to product availability.
  • No cash refunds, bank transfers, or payment reversals will be provided.
  • The replacement will be processed after the returned product has been received and inspected by our team.

Please Note: Abhijatya follows a replacement-only policy. Customers are eligible for a replacement of the same product, not a monetary refund or exchange for a different product.

How to Request a Return or Exchange

Contact our customer support team with the following details:

  • Order ID.
  • Product name.
  • Reason for return or exchange.
  • Mandatory unboxing video.
  • Supporting photographs, if applicable.

Our team will review your request and guide you through the next steps.

Abhijatya reserves the right to decline return or exchange requests that do not meet the applicable eligibility requirements, including requests submitted without the mandatory unboxing video.

Frequently Asked Questions

Orders & Payments

Q. How can I place an order?

A. Browse our jewellery and saree collections, select your favourite product, add it to your shopping bag and proceed to checkout.

Q. What payment methods do you accept?

A. We aim to support UPI, debit cards, credit cards, net banking and other available payment options displayed during checkout.

Q. Will I receive an order confirmation?

A. Yes. Once your order is successfully placed, you will receive an order confirmation through your registered contact details.

Jewellery

Q. Are your jewellery products suitable for everyday wear?

A. Product suitability depends on the material, finish and design. Please refer to the individual product description for details.

Q. How should I care for my jewellery?

A. Keep jewellery away from water, perfumes, chemicals and excessive moisture. Store each piece separately in a dry place.

Q. Will the jewellery look exactly like the photographs?

A. We make every effort to display accurate product images. Slight colour variations may occur due to lighting and screen settings.

Sarees

Q. What types of sarees do you offer?

A. Our collection may include traditional, designer, festive, silk, cotton and occasion-wear sarees, depending on availability.

Q. Does every saree come with a blouse piece?

A. Blouse-piece availability and details are mentioned in the individual product description.

Q. How should I maintain my saree?

A. Care instructions depend on the fabric and embellishments. Please follow the specific care recommendations provided with the product.

Shipping & Delivery

Q. Do you deliver across India?

A. We offer delivery to serviceable locations across India. You can check delivery availability using your pincode at checkout, where supported.

Q. How long does delivery take?

A. Estimated delivery generally takes 5–10 business days after dispatch, depending on your location.

Q. How can I track my order?

A. Once your order is dispatched, tracking information will be shared through your registered contact details.

Returns & Refunds

Q. Can I return or exchange a product?

A. Eligible products may be returned or exchanged according to our Return & Exchange Policy.

Q. What should I do if I receive a damaged product?

A. Contact our support team within 48 hours of delivery with your order number, photographs and an unboxing video.

Q. How long do refunds take?

A. Once a return is approved, refunds are initiated through the applicable payment method. The actual credit time depends on your payment provider.

Customer Support

Q. How can I contact your team?

A. You can reach us through the contact information provided on our Contact Us page.

Q. Can I change my order after placing it?

A. Please contact us as soon as possible. Changes may be possible before dispatch, subject to order status.

Still have questions? We're here to help. Reach out to our customer support team and we'll be happy to assist you.
